## Wins from the flat-aware (flat-subtree) tree path

The flat-subtree fast path triggers whenever `build_table` finds a maximal internal node whose subtree is flat with depth D ≥ 2. In that region the stream switches from D bitmap layers to one N·D-bit packed payload, decoded with `code_to_sym[local_code]` + scatter. Distributions where the path dominates: `sparse_*`, `two_sym_*`, `flat_M*`, `proba80`, `uniform`, `gzip_random`, `bell_s80`.

Headlines:
- Largest absolute throughput: M4 Max sparse_4 at 48 G symbols/s (13.5× huf0_x1, 9.0× huf0_x2 multi-stream).
- Largest ratio: Zen 3 SSE sparse_16 at 22.7× — flat path lets a no-VBMI2 backend match VBMI2 throughput on that distribution.
- AVX-512 flat_M3 at 21.8 G sym/s vs huf0_x1 1.07 G — the VBMI2 vpermb/vpcompressw chain is the single biggest win across platforms for skewed-flat data.
- Graviton 4 sees the same 16-17× on the two_sym/sparse cluster; flat-subtree lifts it from huf0-parity to dominant.

## Where the flat path does NOT activate

Moderate-entropy distributions (`bell_s10/30`, `geometric`, `english`, `zipfian`, `prose_pride`, `html_wiki`, `source_c`, `json_api`, `log_apache`, `csv_numeric`, `chinese_text`) have no maximal flat subtree of depth ≥ 2. On those, current standings are:

| Platform   | Typical ratio vs huf0_x1 | Note                                |
|------------|--------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| M4 Max     | 1.7–2.3×                 | Wins via tree-walk partition alone  |
| Graviton 4 | 0.7–1.0×                 | Loses — partition kernel store-port bound |
| Xeon       | 1.4–1.7×                 | Wins on most; loses html_wiki/prose_pride |
| Zen 3      | 0.6–0.9×                 | Loses — SSE4.1 partition kernel narrow |

This is the open frontier: making tree-walk competitive on real-text distributions without a flat region.
