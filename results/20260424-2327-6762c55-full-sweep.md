Notes:
- **Zen 3's 32% wrapper share is unusually high.**  That's the
  root-level partition + prefill; suggests SSE4.1 `pshufb` partition
  makes the initial tree level disproportionately expensive.
- All platforms are inside the decoder — external overhead is
  negligible, same as before the TBL work.

## Observations per platform

### Apple M4 Max (NEON)

Wins 18/19 distributions (proba14 the only loss at 0.92×, as
expected — analyzer predicted <1% flat-subtree coverage).  Moderate-
entropy set (english, bell_*, proba02, zipfian) all at 1.13× – 1.81×.
flat_M* synthetics at 4.2–4.9×.  Scalar (`pivco_s`) also strong at
2000+ M/s on flat-tree cases via the scalar-path TBL helpers.

### Intel Xeon 6975P-C (AVX-512)

Wins 15/19 (losing only proba14 0.68×, english 1.02× barely,
bell_s10 1.03× barely, Graviton-range workloads).  flat_M3 hits
**11.96×** via `vpshufb` + `vpmultishiftqb`; flat_M5/6 hit **10+×**
via `vpermb` over ymm/zmm.  The "memcpy-split" fix (commits
[3f27e812ba7ab2c9e0998b501475ede86a89637d](../), [7b2fb8d882e6570f1b1e4e226e9c84bf055dbb04](../)) unlocked
D=3/5/6 from earlier regressions.  Still unusual: **bell_s80
2.98×** — the flat-subtree path really sings on D=5/6 heavy
distributions.

### AWS Graviton 4 (Neoverse-V2, NEON)

**Regression on D=5/D=6!**  bell_s80 went from pre-TBL ~1055 M/s to
526 M/s (−50%).  flat_M5 from 1809 → 1282 (−29%).  flat_M6 from
1275 → 1194 (−6%).

Diagnosis hypothesis: Neoverse-V2 has notably lower `vqtbl2q_u8`
(2-reg TBL) and `vqtbl4q_u8` (4-reg TBL) throughput than Apple M4.
On M4 both are ~1/cycle; on V2 they may drop to 1/2 or 1/4 cycle.
The D=5 path uses `vqtbl2` (32B c2s) and D=6 uses `vqtbl4` (64B).
D=2/3/4 (single-register `vqtbl1`) all win on c8g.

This means **the NEON D=5/D=6 paths should probably be gated at
build/runtime on Graviton 4** — or replaced with 2× `vqtbl1` + blend
which might beat the multi-reg TBL on V2.  Logged as a new IDEAS
item.

Remaining Graviton 4 wins: two_sym_* 8.5×, sparse_* 7–8×, proba80
2.2×, uniform 2.5×, flat_M3 3.7×, flat_M7 2.8×.

### AMD EPYC 7R13 (Zen 3 SSE4.1)

Wins 8/19.  sparse_16 **11.66×**, flat_M7 **2.29×**, uniform 2.17×,
proba80 1.12×.  Moderate-entropy cluster (bell_*, proba02, english,
zipfian, geometric) stays at 0.40–0.76× — pure SSE4.1 lacks the
primitives (variable shift, multishift, 32-byte TBL) to bring TBL
wins beyond D=4.  See IDEAS.md §"Revisit SSE4.1 D=2..6" — enabling
AVX2 on the non-AVX-512 build path would likely unlock D=2/3.
