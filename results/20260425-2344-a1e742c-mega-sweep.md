# Mega sweep — 29 distributions × 4 platforms

**Date:** 2026-04-25 23:44 UTC
**Commit:** [a1e742cded9f059d4e165177deca1ac8e26ba49e](../) (tip of main)

**Apple M4 wins all 10 real-world distributions**, and is the *only* platform where the standard text workloads beat the standard Huffman implementations.  Xeon AVX-512 nearly does (`source_c` parity, log/json within ±3% of parity).  Graviton 4 and Zen 3 lose by 25–60% on these workloads.

The `english` synthetic distribution at 1.33× M4 / 1.24× Xeon was overly optimistic — real prose drops M4 by 0.25× and Xeon by 0.34×.  We're keeping `english` in the bench (it tracks the historical cluster) but the per-platform tables now lead with the real data.

## Per-platform commentary

### Apple M4 Max (NEON, 128KB L1D, block 8192) — strong everywhere

`proba14` 1.11×, all real-world data wins (1.08–2.48×), plus the historical sparse / flat / two_sym wins (4.5–10×).  M4 is the only platform where PIVCO is unconditionally the right default — every distribution beats huf0 / trad_4s.

### Intel Xeon 6975P-C (AVX-512 VBMI2, 48KB L1D, block 8192) — good on synthetic, marginal on real prose

Synthetic wins look identical to the cee2366 picture: `bell_s80` 3.33×, flat_M5/6 ~10×, proba14 cross-flipped.  Real-world: `image_jpeg` 1.87× and `gzip_random` 6.53× lead, but `prose_pride` (0.90×), `html_wiki` (0.92×), `json_api` (0.97×) lose to huf0_x2.  AVX-512's `vpcompressw` partition is fast but real prose's deep tree (max_len 15) dominates.

### AWS Graviton 4 (NEON, 64KB L1D, block 8192) — wins synthetic, loses most real text

Most real-world workloads land 0.63–0.92× of huf0_x2.  Only `image_jpeg` and `gzip_random` win.  Diagnosis: Graviton 4 NEON `tbl` throughput is below M4's, and the partition-step count on real text (3.0–3.2 ops/elem) makes the per-element overhead more expensive than huf0_x2's serial table lookup.

### AMD EPYC 7R13 Zen 3 (SSE4.1, 32KB L1D, block 4096) — flat-tree only

Real-world distributions all lose at 0.44–0.66× except `gzip_random` (3.77×, huf0 fails).  Zen 3 SSE4.1 has no `vpcompressw` equivalent, so the partition step is the bottleneck on deep-tree distributions.  This is consistent with the existing IDEAS.md "Zen 3 hybrid block decoder" follow-up — falling back to huf0 on low-flat-coverage tables would close most of the real-world gap on Zen 3.
