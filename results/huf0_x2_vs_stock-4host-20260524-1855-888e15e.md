# huf0 forced-4X2 vs stock HUF_decompress (auto-dispatch)

Decode throughput (opaque, dec_op MB/s), median of 9 invocations per cell.
stock = HUF_decompress auto-dispatch + HUF_compress optimal log (pure defaults).
x2    = forced HUF_decompress4X2, prebuilt CTable @ HUFLOG=11 (PIVCO_MAX_CODE_LEN).
tie   = within +/-3%. Generated 20260524-1855, sha 888e15e.

## Verdict
stock is equal-or-better on every host; never a meaningful loss.
Big stock wins where forced-X2 mispredicts: flat_M6/M7, image_jpeg (x86),
and moderate-entropy text (html/json/log/source/chinese) on all hosts.
On x86, forced-X2 is pathologically slow (~830-1000 MB/s) on flat_M6/M7,
proba02, bell_s30, image_jpeg; auto-dispatch picks X1 and is ~1.3-1.8x faster.
